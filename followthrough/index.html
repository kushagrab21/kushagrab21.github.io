    How I made it

    I built Followthrough in a day, at the AI Tinkerers hackathon with OpenAI in Singapore on 12 September.

    The part I cared about was who gets to say the work is done. One AI makes the edits, plain code collects the evidence (the document read back after saving, the quoted lines looked up in the original), and a second AI sees only that evidence and decides whether to accept the edit, so the screen says “Saved” or “Changes checked” only once the evidence exists.

    The note to the event team is only drafted, it is never sent.
